## Source Quality Ladder

1. Primary technical source: paper, official docs, benchmark report, standards body, code repo, filing.
2. Primary business source: company announcement, investor relations, regulator, court record, patent database.
3. Quantitative market evidence: government statistics, regulator publications, reputable analyst reports, industry benchmark studies, filings, audited surveys, and first-party customer evidence.
4. Independent reputable reporting: Reuters, AP, Bloomberg, Financial Times, The Information, TechCrunch for startup coverage, domain-specific trade publications.
5. Expert commentary: conference talks, analyst notes, well-known researchers, maintainers, practitioners.
6. Social or community signals: X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub discussions. Use as weak signal unless confirmed.
7. SEO summaries, unattributed blogs, copied content. Avoid unless no better source exists.

## Data Gap Language

Use clear uncertainty labels:

- **Verified**: supported by primary or multiple reputable sources.
- **Likely**: supported by reputable secondary sources but missing primary confirmation.
- **Weak signal**: social/community evidence or single-source claim.
- **Unknown**: searched but not found; recommend follow-up.
- **Proxy**: relevant but not a direct match for the requested region, segment, or buyer.
