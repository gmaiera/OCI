# Research Skills

Research skills turn uncertainty into cited analysis that can support a decision.

| Skill | This does that |
|---|---|
| [`deepresearch`](../../Codex/skills/deepresearch) | Produces current, source-backed research briefs and quantified business cases with query decomposition, evidence quality, synthesis, and next steps. |

Use this when a topic is current, niche, expensive to get wrong, or needs primary-source grounding before action.
